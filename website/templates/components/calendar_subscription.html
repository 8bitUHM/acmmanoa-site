Which calendar software would you like to use?

Google Calendar

  1. Copy the feed URL below.
  2. On a computer, open Google Calendar. Next to Other calendars, click + → From URL.
  3. Paste the URL, then click Add calendar.

New subscriptions must be added in a computer web browser, not the Google Calendar mobile app. Once added, the calendar is available in the app.

Apple Calendar on Mac

  1. Click Open Apple Calendar below and confirm the subscription.
  2. Or copy the feed URL, then in Calendar choose File → New Calendar Subscription, paste the URL, and click Subscribe.
  3. Choose a name and color. Select iCloud as the location to see it on your other Apple devices, then click OK.

Apple Calendar on iPhone or iPad

  1. Tap Open Apple Calendar below and follow the subscription prompt.
  2. Or copy the feed URL, then in Calendar choose Calendars → Add Calendar → Add Subscription Calendar.
  3. Paste the URL and tap Find (iOS/iPadOS 26 or later) or Subscribe (18 or earlier).
  4. Choose a name, color, and account, then tap Done or Add. Choose iCloud to see it on your other Apple devices.

Microsoft Outlook (Windows)

  1. Copy the feed URL below and switch to Outlook's Calendar view.
  2. New Outlook: choose Add calendar → Subscribe from web, paste the URL, then select Import.
  3. Classic Outlook: choose Open Calendar → From Internet in the ribbon, paste the URL, and select OK. Confirm the subscription if prompted.

Use the web subscription option, rather than importing a downloaded file, to receive updates.
